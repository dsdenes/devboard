## What transfers

- **Octo Code (dark devtools).** Its palette is effectively the repository's GitHub-dark
  material, so the page keeps the flat `background` → `surface` → `surface-raised` layering and
  1px `border` rules. Lists are full-width rows with `8px 16px`-scale padding and a bottom rule;
  density is treated as a feature, not a compromise. Monospace carries timestamps, counts, and
  identifiers. This supports a 27px cycle row and a fixed-layout table instead of the current
  wrapped, 11px strip.
- **Command Center (security operations).** Status is a word in a badge with one tone; operational
  labels are small uppercase with wider tracking; tables are compact and rely on the header row
  for structure. That maps to the `overline` stat labels and one toned action badge per region.
- **WattVision (dark monitoring dashboard).** KPI values are monospace over muted labels; the
  dashboard is a grid of bounded cards with a large main panel and side panels; alerts pair a
  tinted surface with the error color. That is the reason the newest cycle becomes a compact stat
  rail and the 100-row history becomes a bounded side panel instead of an unbounded page.
